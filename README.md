# Releaf: Revenue-track evidence

## Primary track

**Revenue**

Releaf is a working product for independent psychologists who need a credible online presence but do not have the time, technical confidence, or budget to build and maintain a traditional website.

The strongest scoring opportunities are:

- Signups that reach the first-use event
- Live product quality
- Pain-point severity
- Right to win
- Virality bonus from verified visitors and signups

For scoring, Releaf should define its first-use event as:

> A psychologist completes setup and publishes their website.

An account registration by itself should not count as meaningful product use.

## Revenue rubric: one-line self-score

- **Signups — L2, 20/80:** one real target user, Diva Sridhar, created her own account, tested the core website-building flow, and published her website without the builder operating the account for her.
- **Live product quality — L4, 24/32:** a real target user completed the account-to-publish journey herself, described Releaf as easier and better suited to therapists than general builders such as Wix, and successfully published a working site. The remaining aesthetic limitations keep this below L5.
- **Revenue generated — L1, 0/16:** no product revenue was collected during the week.
- **Waitlist — L1, 0/16:** no separate waitlist was run.
- **Pain-point severity — L3, 4/8:** one named psychologist interview confirmed the problem and gave specific feedback after using Releaf.
- **SOM — L3, 4/8:** 4,608 active clinical psychologists in the Rehabilitation Council of India registry × a planned ₹4,999 annual price = ₹2.30 crore in annual serviceable market value. The price still needs willingness-to-pay testing.
- **Right to win — L4 candidate, 6/8:** prior mental-health technology research, a 92-page thesis, and the working product show direct domain exposure and a clear product insight.
- **Why now — L1, 0/4:** the current website-builder concept could have been built five years ago; no recent unlock should be invented for the submission.
- **Moat and defensibility — L2, 1/4:** the focused workflow, integrations, and design taste are differentiated, but they do not yet create meaningful switching costs or a data advantage.

**Evidence-backed base self-score: 59/176.**

This score should be updated only when new evidence exists. Diva's completed account-to-publish journey qualifies as the first verified signup and first-use event; the next signup threshold is 51 qualifying users for L3.

This feedback should remain in the future roadmap. It is not part of the current V1 scope and should not be described as a shipped feature.

### Product-expectation warning

Diva referred to Substack, Blogspot, and Medium connections. Releaf currently supports Substack and native Releaf posts only. The submission must not claim that Blogspot or Medium integrations are live.

## Interview summary for the submission form

- **Who we interviewed:** one psychologist, Diva Sridhar.
- **How many:** one verified signup and one completed product-feedback interview.
- **What she was doing before Releaf:** the documented workflow relied on Instagram, referrals, word of mouth, and repeated conversations with prospective clients; general website builders required technical and design knowledge she did not feel therapists should need.

## Prior research and domain evidence

The 2021 thesis **“Releaf: A Mental Health Initiative”** provides prior domain research and demonstrates that this problem was not selected only for Build Week.

The research included a survey of 239 respondents and reported:

- 75.7% said they had experienced depressive thoughts.
- Among respondents who had experienced depressive thoughts, 79.8% had never taken therapy.
- Only 20.5% said gaining access to reliable therapists in India was easy.
- 67.4%, or 161 of 239 respondents, said they would be open to using the proposed digital system.
- Among respondents interested in the proposed system, the largest age group was 18–24.

The thesis also observed that mental-health professionals relied on platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn to advertise and reach potential clients. It argued that a dedicated digital platform could improve their reach and marketability.

### Evidence boundary

The thesis survey studied potential users of a broader mental-health platform. It did not interview psychologists about website-building problems. It supports the wider access problem and Releaf’s founder-market insight, but it must not be presented as 239 psychologist interviews or direct validation of the current website builder.

### SOM: L3

**Expected points:** 4  
**Calculation:** `(L3 - 1) × 2 = 4`

#### Bottoms-up calculation

- **Beachhead:** active clinical psychologists listed in the Rehabilitation Council of India Central Rehabilitation Register
- **Active records found:** 4,608
- **Planned annual price:** ₹4,999 per psychologist
- **SOM:** `4,608 × ₹4,999 = ₹23,035,392`, or approximately **₹2.30 crore per year**

The ₹4,999 price is a product assumption, not proven willingness to pay. At the ₹2,000 example session fee used in the current product, it is equal to roughly 2.5 sessions per year. That makes it a plausible price to test, but only real purchases can validate it.

The registry count is a conservative documented starting point rather than the whole market. It includes active RCI-registered clinical psychologists and excludes many counselling psychologists who could also use Releaf. It also does not prove that every listed professional runs an independent practice. For the submission, the precise claim should be: **a ₹2.30 crore documented beachhead among active RCI-registered clinical psychologists**, not the total Indian psychologist market.

**Method:** on 4 September 2026, the public register was searched across all states and clinical-psychology qualifications. It returned 5,328 records, of which 4,608 were marked active. Only the aggregate count was used; no personal registry data was retained in this evidence document.

**Sources:** [Rehabilitation Council of India](https://rehabcouncil.nic.in/) and [Central Rehabilitation Register search](https://rciregistration.nic.in/rehabcouncil/newsearchpage.jsp).

## Submission-ready pain statement

Independent psychologists often attract clients through Instagram, referrals, and word of mouth, but they lack one credible place where a prospective client can understand their qualifications, approach, services, writing, availability, and ways to reach out. General website builders still expect users to make technical, writing, branding, and layout decisions. In our first user interview, psychologist Diva Sridhar confirmed this gap: “Not all therapists are well versed in building websites or understanding tech language and script.” After using Releaf, she described it as easy to navigate, complete enough to cover the essentials, and quick to use.

## Submission-ready right-to-win statement

Releaf builds on domain work that began in 2021 with a 92-page thesis on digital mental-health access and a survey of 239 respondents. That research showed both difficulty accessing reliable therapists and openness to digital mental-health systems. Build Week narrowed the original broad platform idea into a safer, more focused product: a website builder that helps independent psychologists explain their practice, publish educational content, connect booking, and receive enquiries without becoming a clinical-record system or therapy platform.

## The one honest paragraph

Releaf is a guided website builder for independent psychologists who rely on Instagram, referrals, and repeated conversations to explain their practice. A psychologist signs up, adds their professional details, chooses the sections and visual direction, connects existing writing and Calendly if they use it, configures an enquiry form, and publishes one shareable website without starting from a blank canvas. They care because prospective clients can understand their qualifications, approach, services, fees, writing, and availability before deciding whether to reach out. The working product is live at [growthx-build-week-chi.vercel.app](https://growthx-build-week-chi.vercel.app/).
